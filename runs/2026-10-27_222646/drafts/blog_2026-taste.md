Our project investigates the attention heads that causally produce refusal in small instruct LLMs using activation patching, ablation, and LoRA editing. We specifically mapped the refusal circuit, demonstrating that the relevant heads are editable rather than merely deletable. Our findings indicate that the edit is shallow and that a benign-only training approach can transfer to harmful compliance, effectively jailbreaking well-aligned models without harmful training data.

This connects to your benchmark’s evaluation of safety research proposals, particularly regarding the validity of safety interventions. We are interested in whether your evaluation metrics distinguish between superficial refusals and substantive safety judgments.
